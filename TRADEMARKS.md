# Trademarks

The code in this repository is Apache-2.0. **The Nobble name and logo are not**, and Apache-2.0 says
so itself: §6 grants no rights in the licensor's trade marks.

What that means in practice, in the two directions people actually ask about:

**You may** say your addon works with Nobble, or is for Nobble. Describing what your software is
compatible with is nominative use and needs no permission. Call it what you like — `nobble-addon-foo`
is the filename convention the daemon requires, and using it is not a claim to the name.

**You may not** name your project, product or company Nobble, use the logo as your own, or present an
addon in a way that suggests it is published or endorsed by Nobble when it is not.

This file is a plain-language summary of the position, not a licence. If you want to use the marks in
a way this does not cover, ask.
